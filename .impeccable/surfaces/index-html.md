# Surface — the piece (single explorable surface)

**Scope.** The whole work: one WebGL surface, no sub-routes yet.
**Visitor mode.** Experience — the artifact leads, the interface recedes.

**Audience & job.** Hiring managers and design directors arriving from a shared link with
tabs open; five-second bar on the first viewport. Secondarily Juan, for whom this is also
a passion project. Success is conversations, not traffic.

**Action.** There is no CTA. The visitor's job is to move inward and find something.

**Content.** Twelve moments, thematic not chronological, split by the shrine's own two
states: *te pido* (2006, 2007, 2010, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2018) and *gracias por el favor
concedido* (2021, 2022), plus the shirt itself. The proportion — overwhelmingly petition —
is load-bearing and must stay legible. Maracanã appears in both states, 2014 and 2021.

**Direction.** El Santuario (seed 714794b2, chosen as the pick card).
**Composition.** C — Las Estampitas. Approved comp:
`.impeccable/mocks/comp-c-las-estampitas.html` (+ `.json` sidecar).
Volumetric field, no fixed order, panels suspended at depth, movement in any direction.

**Memorable moment.** The visitor carries the light. Panels resolve only as they are
approached, and celeste — withheld from the entire palette — arrives only at a release.

**Constraints carried from the roll.** One shared clock for all motion; a visitor ledger;
asymmetry and damage as law; one gate that must be earned.

**Unresolved.**
- Image ladder: each panel declares its own medium (estampita / painted ex-voto / bare
  plaque / photoreal). Deliberately undecided by the user; loader must stay null-safe.
- Bilingual EN/ES parity — binding on the sibling project, unconfirmed here.
- The no-WebGL fallback for a volumetric field. Genuinely unsolved.
- Named build risk: a sparse opening is the composition most exposed to
  "reverence that reads as cold", which the user listed as a failure condition.
