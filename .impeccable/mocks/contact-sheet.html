EL SANTUARIO · THREE COMPOSITIONS

The world is settled. What is being decided here is the shape of the space.
FIXED ACROSS ALL THREE — warm-black ground · Gauchito red as the committed carrier · candle-warm key light · brass = gracias, tin = te pido · celeste withheld from the entire palette until a release is earned · Chivo for engraved plaque capitals, Alegreya for votive text — both Buenos Aires foundries, neither used on La Bombonera
Composition A
La Pared / the wall
One vast wall of plaques running past both edges of the frame. You move laterally along it, and you carry the light — a candle pool that reveals five or six plaques at a time out of hundreds.
Topology lateral
Density extreme — the mass is the message
Focal the single brass plaque inside the pool
First move drag sideways into the dark
The proportion argument is strongest here: 214 petitions against 9 thanks is legible as pure texture before a word is read. But lateral movement is the closest of the three to a scroll, which is the thing you said must not happen.
Composition B
El Árbol / the tree
The shrine as one mass you orbit. A tree hung with hundreds of red ribbons, plaques wired into the canopy, and the offerings — bottles, tin charms, candles — heaped at the base. Nothing is behind you; you circle it.
Topology radial — you orbit
Density massed into one silhouette
Focal the red canopy itself
First move drag to rotate around it
The most arresting first viewport by a wide margin, and the most literally Argentine — this is what a Gauchito Gil shrine actually looks like. Risk: one object means one idea, and the twelve moments have to earn their place on a tree without becoming decorations hung on a concept.
Composition C
Las Estampitas / the field
No wall and no centre. The moments are votive panels suspended at different depths in a dark volume, and you move through them in any direction — the only one of the three with no implied path at all.
Topology volumetric — six degrees
Density sparse, each panel legible
Focal the one lit gracias panel
First move move inward, toward anything
Truest to "no fixed order," and the panels are already shaped to hold your image ladder — note the third card reads SIN IMAGEN, which is the null-safe slot showing its real behaviour. Risk: without a wall or a mass, the field can read as floating cards in space, which is where a lot of WebGL work goes to die.
These are rendered HTML and canvas at 1440×900, not image-model comps — this session has no image generation, so what you are approving is something buildable rather than a picture of it. Tell me which composition, what to carry across from the others, and anything that feels false to the shrine. The image ladder stays open either way: every panel declares its own medium and the loader is null-safe, so estampita, painted ex-voto, bare plaque, or photoreal can be decided panel by panel later.
